FAQ

Before starting a project.

Short answers for small businesses that need a website without technical confusion.

What kind of websites do you build?

I build landing pages, WordPress websites and business-focused websites for service brands, local businesses, studios, restaurants and creators.

Can you redesign an existing website?

Yes. I can improve layout, first screen, mobile experience, section structure, buttons and overall visual presentation.

Can the website connect to Booksy, WhatsApp, Instagram or email?

Yes. The site can include direct buttons to Booksy, WhatsApp, Instagram, email, phone, Google Maps or a contact form.

Do you work through freelance platforms?

Yes. You can contact me directly, or review my Upwork, Useme and LinkedIn profiles before starting a project.

Do you only build the exact niches shown in the portfolio?

No. The portfolio shows range. The same structure can be adapted to consulting, legal, accounting, cleaning services, beauty, tattoo, detailing, restaurants and other service businesses.

How does the order process work?

You choose a package, send a short request, and I reply with scope questions, timeline and final estimate before the project starts.

What payment methods are available?

PayPal Invoice is the main payment option. Useme is available on request, especially if you prefer a platform-based workflow or need a more formal contractor process. Upwork payment can be used if the project starts through Upwork.
