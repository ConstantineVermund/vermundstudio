Project Terms

These are general terms for starting a website project with Vermund Studio. Final terms are confirmed after scope review.

Scope first

Before any project starts, the required pages, sections, content, platform, timeline and delivery format are clarified.

Starting prices

Prices shown on the website are starting points. Final pricing depends on page count, content volume, CMS needs, integrations and revision scope.

Client materials

The client is expected to provide business information, services, prices, images, contact details, booking links and any existing brand materials when available.

Revisions

Reasonable revision rounds can be included depending on the package and agreed scope. Major scope changes may require a new estimate.

Payment

Payment workflow is discussed after the scope is confirmed. PayPal Invoice is the main direct payment option. Useme is available on request, especially for platform-based contractor workflow. Upwork payment can be used when the project starts through Upwork.

Delivery

Delivery may include static website files, WordPress setup, project archive, GitHub Pages deployment guidance or handoff instructions depending on the project.
