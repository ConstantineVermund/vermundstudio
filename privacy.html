Privacy Policy

This page explains how Vermund Studio handles information submitted through the project request form, Tally form embed or direct contact links.

Information collected

The project request form may include your name, business type, email address, preferred contact method, current website or business link, selected website type, budget range and project details.

How information is used

This information is used only to reply to your request, clarify the project scope, prepare an estimate and discuss next steps.

Third-party links

The website uses an embedded Tally form for project requests and may link to Upwork, Useme, LinkedIn, GitHub Pages or portfolio projects. Those platforms have their own privacy policies.

Form processing

This GitHub Pages version uses Tally to collect project requests. Vermund Studio uses submitted information only to reply, clarify project scope and prepare an estimate.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact Vermund Studio through the project form or listed professional profiles.
